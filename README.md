# CIVIA — Your city, made understandable.

**An interactive, mobile-first environmental intelligence product concept.**

CIVIA helps people interpret the conditions around them and make small, informed decisions about their day. It brings weather, air quality and personalized guidance into a calm, accessible interface.

> **Portfolio / concept project:** Not affiliated with a government or public-health organization. Live environmental information is provided by Open-Meteo; personalized guidance is **deterministic and rules-based** in this version, not generated by a language model. Any fallback sample data is labeled. No study participants, product outcomes or uplift metrics are claimed.

## Product thinking

**Problem:** Environmental information is scattered across technical dashboards and difficult to translate into action. Rather than showing more metrics, can we help someone answer: *What does this mean for my day?*

**Design principles**
1. **Meaning before metrics** — an actionable summary, then supporting data.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — clear overview first; chart and methodology when wanted.
3. **Honest intelligence** — show the factors behind guidance and never imply a language model or measured accuracy if one is not present.
4. **Trust and control** — explicit source, live/sample status, optional location access and local preferences.
5. **Accessibility by default** — keyboard operation, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, readable labels and responsive layouts.

**Core journey:** Select a city → understand today's conditions → explore how they change → examine a contextual insight → adjust personal priorities.

## Stack

## Data and privacy

- Weather: [Open-Meteo Forecast API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs)
- Air quality: [Open-Meteo Air Quality API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api), using **US AQI** (not a hyperlocal measured sensor).
- Air-quality model resolution and forecast limitations vary by region; avoid treating point forecasts as street-level measurements.
- Location access is optional and requires explicit browser permission.
- City and preference choices are kept in browser local storage; no account or analytics SDK.
- Data calls from the browser go directly to Open-Meteo. Check Open-Meteo terms and attribution requirements before any commercial reuse.

## Product-design case framing

This is an **independently designed product concept**, not a deployed customer product or validated research study.

**Hypotheses to test:** People can identify an appropriate next step faster with a contextual summary than with separate raw metrics; evidence disclosure increases perceived confidence; user-controlled priorities improve the relevance of suggestions.

**Research plan:** Five moderated comprehension tests with a mix of people familiar and unfamiliar with AQI; compare raw metrics vs. contextual summary; assess task success, comprehension errors, time to first action, perceived confidence and accessibility.

**Proposed product metrics (not actual results):** Insight comprehension, action relevance rating, opt-out rate, repeat visits and data-source transparency interaction.

## Future scope

If this concept evolves: integrate a vetted LLM behind an audited server endpoint, with deterministic data grounding, optional user prompts, uncertainty disclosure, privacy boundaries and measurable evaluation. Until then, the interface is intentionally honest about rule-based guidance.
